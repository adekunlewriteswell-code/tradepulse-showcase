# TradePulse

**A demand-intelligence concept for understanding the market before committing distribution resources.**

> **Public concept and prototype overview.** This repository documents the idea and product direction. It is not a public release of the private working prototype or any operational field dataset.

## The question behind TradePulse

Traditional sales systems are good at showing what happened **after** a visit, order, or sale. TradePulse explores a different question:

**Where is demand beginning to form—and what evidence supports acting on it?**

The aim is to help route-to-market teams see early market signals before making expensive decisions about truck deployment, outlet coverage, stock allocation, or field attention.

## Product direction

- **Lightweight signal capture:** Reduce the effort required for frontline contributors to report useful market observations.
- **Market intelligence views:** Organize signals by place, product, and time so patterns become more visible.
- **Demand groups:** Bring related signals together instead of treating each contribution as an isolated event.
- **Contributor visibility:** Make it possible to inspect the observations behind a demand picture.
- **Accessible participation:** Explore low-bandwidth and USSD-compatible submission approaches alongside digital workflows.

Some of these ideas are present in demo/prototype workflows; others are product directions under exploration.

## How it differs from field-sales software

| Field-sales execution systems | TradePulse concept |
| --- | --- |
| Focus on assigned visits, transactions, and execution | Focus on emerging demand signals and their evidence |
| Describe activities carried out in the market | Explore opportunities before deployment decisions |
| Help manage an active sales operation | Help inform where attention may be valuable next |

TradePulse is intended as a **standalone demand-intelligence product**, not a rebranded RouteCRM feature.

## Stage and limitations

**Prototype and exploration.** The value, accuracy, contribution incentives, data quality, and operational usefulness of these signals require field validation. A demo is not evidence of a completed production deployment.

No customer identities, confidential commercial metrics, proprietary code, or private access details are included here.
